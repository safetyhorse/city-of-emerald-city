Announcements

Yellow Brick Road resurfacing on the West Approach

Posted September 12, 2011

The West Approach between Poppy Field and the City Gate will have single-lane access on weekdays from 7 a.m. to 4 p.m. through mid-October. Travelers are asked to use the North Path as an alternate route. Access to residences will be maintained.

Annual Emerald Festival returns October 3

Posted September 8, 2011

The Emerald Festival returns to Central Plaza with food vendors, live music, and a lantern parade down the Yellow Brick Road. Road closures will be in effect from noon to 10 p.m. Free shuttle service will run from the outer districts.
